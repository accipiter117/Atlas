# LPC test warrior: credits

Sheet: `assets/sprites/lpc-test-warrior.png`, composed for Atlas from five layers of the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator, animations walk, thrust, slash and hurt, arranged in the classic LPC layout (thrust rows 4 to 7, walk rows 8 to 11, slash rows 12 to 15, hurt row 20).
